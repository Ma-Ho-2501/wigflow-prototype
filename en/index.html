WigFlow Prototype

From references and specs to quotes, progress updates, final approval, and shipping.

A tool for commission-based wig makers to manage every order — from intake to delivery — in one place, instead of scattered across spreadsheets, DMs, and one-off forms.

By sharing specs, pricing, and progress directly with clients, it aims to cut down on miscommunication and missed approvals, so makers can focus on the work with peace of mind.

Try both the maker's order dashboard and the client-facing status page — fully interactive.

Spend 1–2 minutes clicking around, then a 30-second feedback form at the end.

*All client names, character names, and amounts shown are fictional demo data.
*This is not an app available on the App Store — it's an interactive prototype of a concept still in development.
